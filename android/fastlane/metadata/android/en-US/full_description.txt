Gozdar is a field app for forest owners, contractors, and forestry professionals working in Slovenia.

Plan and manage work directly on your phone:

- view forest parcels on an interactive map
- use cadastral and aerial layers for orientation in the field
- record logs with dimensions, volume, species, and notes
- save important locations for later navigation
- keep parcel and logging data together in one place

The app is designed for practical outdoor use, with fast access to map tools, parcel data, and log tracking while you work.

Whether you are checking parcels, planning cuts, or keeping a clear record of timber on site, Gozdar helps you stay organized in the forest and on the move.
