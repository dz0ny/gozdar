Gozdar is a field app for forest owners, loggers and foresters in Slovenia and Croatia. Parcels, cadastre, logs and navigation are in one place and keep working where there is no signal.

MAP AND CADASTRE
- aerial imagery, parcel boundaries with labels, forest stands and more than 40 layers from ZGS and GURS
- long-press the map to import a parcel from the cadastre
- find a parcel by number, cadastral municipality, address or owner from public registers
- cadastral data for Slovenia and Croatia

FOREST
- keep your parcels with area, forest type, notes and allowed cut
- generalised parcel value from GURS, with site quality and forest accessibility
- a separate layer shows parcels currently in court auctions

LOGS
- enter diameter and length, Gozdar calculates the volume
- logs are grouped by tree species
- totals in m³, stacked cubic metres (PRM) and loose cubic metres (NM)

FELLING AND NAVIGATION
- mark trees for felling on the map and send them to the logger with one link
- the compass shows direction and distance to a boundary point or saved location

WITHOUT SIGNAL
- maps you have viewed are saved automatically, larger areas can be downloaded in advance
- cadastre and search work offline once the data is downloaded
- copy maps and data to a colleague directly between phones

EXPORT
- logs to Excel, a parcel with its map to PDF, parcels to KML for other GIS software

Everything you enter stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics.

DATA SOURCES
Gozdar is not an official app of any government body and does not represent any government entity. It shows public data from these sources:
- Surveying and Mapping Authority of the Republic of Slovenia (GURS): https://www.e-prostor.gov.si
- Slovenia Forest Service (ZGS): https://prostor.zgs.gov.si
- State Geodetic Administration of Croatia (DGU): https://oss.uredjenazemlja.hr

Parcel, ownership and value data is informational and may be out of date. For official data, contact the responsible authority.
