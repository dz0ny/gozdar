# UX review — iOS simulator walkthrough (2026-10-31)

Device: iPhone 17 Pro simulator, debug build `2026.1029.2 (103)`, Slovenian locale, dark theme.
Covered: every tab, parcel detail + editor, log entry, save/load batches, batch detail, map layers,
parcel search, long-press parcel sheet, owner filter, About, and the full 9-page intro wizard.
**Not opened this round:** compass/navigation dialog, tile download dialog, saved locations,
*Dražbe* search tab, notes editor, owner edit, *+ Posek m³* / *Drevo* entry, species management,
the *Dodaj* submenu.

## P1 — broken or misleading

1. **Parcel editor opens on a blank grey map.** Imagery only appears after zooming out once. The cause
   isn't confirmed: `maxNativeZoom` is already set (`parcel_editor.dart:294`), so look into it before choosing a fix.
2. **Settings are hidden behind an easter egg.** The About screen, which holds language, offline
   kataster regions, storage toggles and support, opens only after tapping a tab 3× (or from the
   kataster update banner). Nothing in the UI points to it (`main_scaffold.dart:214`).
3. **The terms page can be skipped.** "Preskoči" is shown on every page, including the final *Pogoji uporabe*;
   skipping on any page completes onboarding (`intro_wizard_screen.dart:47`, `:67`).
4. **Mixed decimal separators.** The number keypad types `,` but every result shows `.`
   (`6.07 ha`, `0.385 m³`, `0.59 PRM`), while money uses Slovenian format (`42.700 €`, `0,70 €/m²`).
   Dates are mixed too: `29. 10. 2026`, `31. 10. 2026 10:49` and ISO `2026-10-25` (About → offline kataster).
5. **Typo** in `app_sl.arb:1876` `introWelcomeBody`: "belezite" → "beležite".

## P2 — friction

**Gozd tab**
- KO group headers truncate the most useful part: "BABNA POLICA (16…" cuts off the KO number.
- Parcel titles repeat the KO name that's already in the group header ("BABNO POLJE - 746/1" under "BABNO POLJE").
- Owner line: grey all-caps on dark green has low contrast.
- Adding a parcel from this tab is only possible via ⋮ → *Uvozi KML* (or the empty-state import); search/draw need a long-press on the map.
- The person icon in the app bar is an owner **filter** but looks like an account/profile button.
- Owner filter sheet has no drag handle, unlike the other sheets, and uses harsh white dividers.

**Parcel detail**
- The map preview takes ~45% of the first screen; the hero stat shows "23 točk" (vertex count), which means nothing to an owner.
- The most-used actions (*Navigiraj*, *Poglej na karti*) are at the very bottom of a long scroll.
- Two competing filled primary buttons (*+ Posek m³* and *Navigiraj*).
- Zero values are highlighted in arbitrary colours (orange `0.00 m³`, green `0`).
- Horizontal insets don't match: map and buttons at 16 pt, cards at 20 pt.
- The owner card repeats the person icon (header and row); dividers are bright white throughout.

**Parcel editor**
- Dense vertex markers overlap into unreadable stacks (10–21).
- "Počisti" wraps onto two lines in the bottom bar; "Nazaj" is ambiguous (undo vs. back).
- The forest-type segmented control doesn't span the full width.

**Hlodi tab**
- The diameter/length fields have no label or placeholder; only units sit next to them.
- No visible input bound on diameter (354 cm typed with no hint; not submitted).
- The numeric keypad has no Next/Done key to move from diameter to length.
- The "Dodano: 1" confirmation is squeezed between "Volumen =" and "— m³", which looks like a layout bug. It also stays on screen.
- Preview precision (0.385) differs from the list (0.38).
- "Vrsta ni izbrana" reads as status but is the species picker. It opens a sheet titled *Pretvorba volumna*, which buries species inside conversion settings. Picking species "Bukev" doesn't preselect PRM factor "Bukev/Hrast".
- After saving, the working list clears with only a "Shranjeno" snackbar: no hint where the logs went, no "open batch" action. You also can't link a batch to a parcel.
- The snackbar is light-on-dark-app (off-theme).
- Naming drifts: *Shrani hlode* vs *Shranjene meritve*.

**Batch detail**
- Date shown three times (name, card, list sheet); the date row is centred while everything else is left-aligned.
- Adding uses a FAB → sheet here but inline entry on the main tab.
- *Delete* sits right next to *Edit* in the app bar (it does confirm, but it's easy to hit by accident).

**Karta**
- The status bar sits on raw imagery with no scrim, so the time and battery are unreadable on bright tiles.
- Opens at very high zoom on blurry over-zoomed imagery.
- Cadastral labels collide heavily at mid zoom ("6634/6", "12412").
- Parcel colours (red / orange / blue / green) have no legend. Your saved parcels *are* green-filled (`map_tab.dart:2355`), but the queried-parcel preview is green too, which muddies the meaning.
- Long-press sheet: four action buttons in four unrelated colours. *Dodaj* in red reads as destructive. KO is written "KO 1651 BABNO POLJE" here vs "BABNO POLJE (1651)" elsewhere.

**Layers sheet**
- Text-only tiles with generic icons; no thumbnails. Each tile is very tall.
- International maps are listed before Slovenian ones.
- The selected tile is reddish-brown (clashes with the palette).
- Overlays tab: content inset 20 pt vs 16 pt elsewhere, and the import button overflows on the right.
- *Uvozi geodatke* is the loudest element in a layer picker.

**Search**
- The hint appears twice (helper text and empty state).
- Searching "746" doesn't surface the user's own 746/1 and 746/2.
- Result trailing icons (target vs +) are unexplained. No recent searches.

**About**
- Called "O aplikaciji" but it's really Settings.
- Offline-kataster summary is one run-on string (regions · count · size · date · status).
- English "Offline" in a Slovenian UI.
- Both storage toggles default to off in an app meant for the field.

**Onboarding**
- 9 pages is long; the page indicator jumps during transitions.
- The long-press page lists the *Dodaj* submenu items (točka / hlodovina / sečnja) as if they were
  the top level; the real sheet shows *Dodaj / Merjenje / Navigiraj / Uvozi* first.
- Doesn't reuse the (good) app icon.
- The terms page uses an off-palette indigo shield.

## Found in code review (Codex, not seen in the simulator)

- **Notes can be lost.** Closing the notes sheet with ✕ discards edits without asking, and the sheet has no
  keyboard inset or scrolling, so the keyboard can cover *Shrani* (`notes_editor_sheet.dart:34–56`).
- **Saved batch logs can't be corrected.** Rows show a tap ripple but `onTap: () {}` does nothing; you can only
  delete (`batch_detail_screen.dart:396`).
- **Navigation shows stale readings on GPS loss.** Position errors only go to debug output, so the last position
  stays on screen. It needs a lost-signal state and the age of the reading (`navigation_compass_dialog.dart:82–108`).
- **Offline tile download scope is opaque.** It downloads from the current zoom to each layer's max and shows only
  a tile count: no size estimate, area preview or detail choice (`tile_download_dialog.dart:149`, `:180`).
- **Auctions confuse an error with no results.** `snapshot.hasError` isn't checked; failure, an empty load and no match
  all show the same message, with no Retry (`parcel_search_dialog.dart:313`, `:333`).
- **Species management misreports.** Removing the last species restores the defaults on reload; re-adding an existing
  species still says "added" (`species_service.dart:19`, `:38`; `species_management_sheet.dart:82`).
- **Log rows can overflow** with large text or long species names: volume, dimensions and species share one
  inflexible row (`log_card.dart:42`).

## Cross-cutting

- **No single accent role.** Tan, green, orange, red, teal, blue and indigo all appear as accents.
- **All-caps cadastral strings everywhere** (KO, owners, parcel names) hurt scanability; title-case them for display.
- **Inconsistent inset / radius / divider tokens** between cards, buttons and sheets.
- **Long-press is the only entry point** for core creation flows.

## Proposed design direction (ordered by leverage)

1. **One accent role.** Tan = primary/interactive. Green = forest / your own data. Orange = warning only.
   Red = destructive only. Remove teal, blue and indigo. Long-press sheet: four neutral tiles, one filled.
   Posek card: zero values in neutral text, colour only when non-zero.
2. **Expose settings and creation.** Rename *O aplikaciji* → *Nastavitve* (About becomes a section inside)
   and open it from a gear in the Gozd app bar; keep the 3-tap only as a dev shortcut. Gozd gets a
   "+ Parcela" FAB (search/import or draw) and an empty-state CTA. Swap the person icon for a filter icon.
3. **Parcel detail: compact header, actions on top.** Header = name · area · KO · owner (drop "23 točk").
   Pin [Navigiraj] [Na karti] [+ Posek] under the app bar. Cap the map preview at ~200 pt.
   Use one 16 pt inset for everything.
4. **Hlodi entry form.** Floating labels *Premer (cm)* / *Dolžina (m)*; keyboard action Next → Done;
   sane ranges with an inline hint. The species chip looks like a chip (caret) and opens its own small sheet;
   conversion factors stay under ⋮ → Pretvorba. Confirmation as a snackbar; preview and list use the same precision.
5. **One `Formats` util.** `NumberFormat.decimalPattern('sl')` + `DateFormat('d. M. yyyy')` everywhere
   (decimal comma, thousands dot). Fixes P1 #4 on every screen.
6. **Map.** ~60 pt top gradient scrim behind the status bar. Give the queried-parcel preview its own colour
   so green always means "my parcel"; add a legend in the layers sheet. Investigate the editor's grey
   initial view (likely the initial zoom vs. imagery availability) before fixing it.
7. **Unified sheets via `sheet_parts.dart`.** Every sheet gets `SheetHandle` + `SheetHeader`. The owner filter
   and overlays tab move to the 16 pt inset. Dividers at ~12 % white.
8. **Display casing.** A `toDisplayCase()` helper for KO and owner names in list/detail views; raw strings stay in data.
9. **Onboarding → 4 pages.** Welcome · Karta (long-press, generated from the actual menu keys) · Hlodi ·
   Offline + terms. No *Preskoči* on the terms page; *Začni* implies acceptance.
10. **Editor.** Hide vertex numbers below zoom ~17; *Počisti* icon-only; *Nazaj* → *Razveljavi* if it's undo.

### Where Codex disagrees (and I agree)

- **Map colours:** keep distinct styles where they carry meaning (measurement, import, selection, own parcels)
  and add line patterns plus a legend. A thick green fill would hide the imagery. The "one accent" rule applies to UI chrome, not map data.
- **Parcel actions:** don't pin three equal buttons. Keep one primary visible and the rest in a compact row;
  check which action is used most before making *Navigiraj* the primary.
- **Keep *Počisti* labelled.** It destroys work; fix the bottom-bar layout instead of making it icon-only.
- **No automatic title case for owners.** It mangles initials and organisation names (`J.P."HRVATSKE CESTE"`);
  improve hierarchy and spacing instead. KO names are safer to title-case.
- **Formatting:** locale-aware, but precision chosen per quantity (area, dimensions, volume); keep times where they identify records.
- **Terms:** dropping *Preskoči* from the last page isn't enough. If acceptance matters, make it a separate required step.

### Combined top 5 (impact ÷ effort)

1. Expose Settings and parcel search/add from Gozd (gear + FAB).
2. Protect note edits and keep *Shrani* above the keyboard.
3. Inline log entry: labels, Next/Done, validation, snackbar confirmation.
4. Shared `sl` number/date formatting.
5. Edit saved batch logs; GPS-lost state in navigation.

**Quick wins (a few lines each):** the typo, dropping *Preskoči* from the terms page (#9),
and the shared number/date formatting (#5).
